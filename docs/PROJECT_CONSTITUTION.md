# Project Constitution

## Product Identity

BWIP is the Bhoodevi Warehouse Intelligence Platform.

It is an AI-powered Warehouse Demand Intelligence and Deal Management Platform.

## Core Rule

BWIP is not a Warehouse Management System.

Do not build inventory, ERP, billing, gate pass, labor management, or warehouse operations features unless they directly support warehouse demand discovery or leasing deal closure.

## Mission

Help warehouse owners, brokers, and business development teams discover genuine warehouse clients, qualify opportunities, match warehouses, manage follow-ups, and close leasing deals.

## Guiding Question

Every module must answer:

"Does this help discover warehouse demand or close warehouse leasing deals?"

If the answer is no, do not build it.
