# AI Context

## AI Vision

BWIP should eventually behave like an AI business development executive for warehouse leasing.

The AI should help users discover, qualify, prioritize, and convert real warehouse leasing opportunities.

## Data Principle

The platform must be designed for real-world verified data sources, not fake data.

Target source types include:

- Official company websites
- Government data
- Logistics information
- Import/export databases
- News
- Company announcements
- Job postings
- Trade directories
- Manufacturing databases
- Industrial parks
- Verified business information

## Intelligence Goals

The AI should eventually detect and reason about:

- Company expansion
- Logistics movement
- New factories
- Distributor expansion
- Import/export growth
- New product launches
- Hiring activity
- Warehouse requirements
- Decision makers
- Opportunity score
- Warehouse match quality
- Outreach recommendations
- Deal closure probability
